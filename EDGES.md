# Backtest: do "Today's edges" predict runs?

_Generated by `scripts/backtest_edges.py` — rerun it to refresh. Read-only: changes no data._

Every regular-season game of 2023–2026 (9718 games, 19436 team-games) is rebuilt from what
was known **before** it: the real batting orders and starters (MLB box scores), each batter's exact
season-to-date FanGraphs lines up to the end of the previous month plus his previous-season line (the
site's Round G1 math), and each starter's score — both as of Opening Day and as the live site would have
shown it that month (rebuilt from his stats so far with the live weights). The edge is then compared
with the runs that offense scored.

## Verdict

**What the site does with this:** the edges card labels each game from the starter's score only: "Tough matchup" for 90+ (the Elite tier), "Slight edge" for 73.5–90; lineup wRC+ is shown as context. Plain scores mean the same in every season (a percentile among active pitchers); the season's "middle starter" does not (2023: 76.0 + 26.2 = 102.2; 2024: 74.1 + 22.8 = 96.9; 2025: 72.3 + 25.4 = 97.7; 2026: 62.5 + 27.4 = 89.9), which is why the site uses plain scores rather than a yardstick.

- **The starter matchup predicts, every season.** Offenses facing a Tough-matchup starter scored **3.81** runs (3.70 to 3.91), Slight 4.30, others 4.69 (league 4.48). Starter score vs runs: in-season 0.114 (0.099 to 0.127), Opening Day only 0.102 (0.087 to 0.118).
- **The lineup matters a little, far less than the starter.** Across all games it shows almost nothing: raw runs 0.011 (-0.006 to 0.027), park-adjusted 0.012 (-0.004 to 0.029). Comparing the two offenses within the same game (same park, weather and day) shows a small real effect, 0.061 (0.039 to 0.083), about half the starter's in those games, 0.129 (0.107 to 0.153). Even with hindsight (full-season lines) a lineup predicts one game's runs at only 0.085, so the site keeps it as context, not in the label.

## Limits

- The lineup also faces the bullpen, which the edge ignores.
- Park factors come from the same seasons' runs (a standard shortcut; the same-game test needs none).
- In-season starter scores use stats through the end of the previous month, so they lag up to a month.
- 95% ranges resample whole team-months (870), or for same-game tests whole park-months.
